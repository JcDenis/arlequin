Display the selector
Add the widget "Theme switcher" to a sidebar from the Widgets page. Visitors then choose their theme; it is remembered in a cookie.
Model
Pick a predefined model or write your own HTML. Switcher: %1$s current page URL, %2$s the items. Item: %1$s page URL, %2$s and %3$s URL parameter, %4$s theme name, %5$s description, %6$s theme id.
Excluded themes
Theme ids (folder names) hidden from visitors, separated by semicolons. Ex: customCSS;berlin.
